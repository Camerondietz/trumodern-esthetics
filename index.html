FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I book an appointment?

All bookings are managed through our secure Square portal. Click any “Book” button to view live availability, select a service, and reserve your time.

What should I do before my first facial?

Arrive with clean skin if possible, avoid retinol or strong actives 48 hours prior, and bring a list of your current products. We’ll handle the rest.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice. Cancellations with less than 24 hour notice and no show appointments may be subject to a 30% service fee.

What is your late policy?

Arrivals over 10 minutes late may need to be rescheduled and will count as a cancellation. Accommondations may be made, but can't extend into the next clients time.

What is your reschedule policy?

You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your next appointment
